Evaluator lens

Assignment Deconstructed: What H TO H Partners Is Actually Testing

The thresholds are filters. The deeper test is whether you can produce causal, reproducible evidence and explain its limits under pressure.

The evaluator’s question

A quant research intern turns an economic idea into a falsifiable experiment, implements it without leaking future information, and communicates what the evidence does and does not support. A spectacular in-sample curve can be created accidentally through timing errors, repeated search, omitted costs, or a biased universe. Hidden data asks the harder question: did the process discover something stable, or merely memorize one history?

The live modification matters for the same reason. If you understand the code and accounting, you can predict the effect of a changed lag or rebalance rule before pressing Enter, isolate an off-by-one bug, and explain unexpected output. Copied logic usually fails that test.

What the thresholds do—and do not prove

Sharpe > 1 / 1.9

A compact reward-to-variability screen under the harness definition. It does not prove significance, eliminate tail risk, or guarantee persistence.

Drawdown limits

A historical path constraint. The worst future drawdown can exceed the observed maximum, especially in a regime absent from training.

Reference strategy

A benchmark for feasibility, not a recipe. Beating it in-sample after many trials may be weaker evidence than a simpler idea with a stable plateau.

Hidden window

A one-time approximation to deployment. Its value collapses if you repeatedly use feedback from it to choose designs.

End-to-end task checklists

Task 1

  1. Audit bar timestamps, short sessions, and warm-up.
  2. Write a causal target-position function.
  3. Verify close decision → next-open fill with a micro-case.
  4. Separate cash, carry, roll, trading cost, and spot P&L.
  5. Inspect turnover, exposure, years, delays, costs, and sensitivity.
  6. State economic rationale and plausible failure regimes.

Task 2

  1. Audit listing histories, liquidity, and the survivor-selected universe.
  2. Create point-in-time eligibility and ranking.
  3. Respect order expiry, execution order, caps, and available cash.
  4. Inspect concentration, turnover, trade attribution, and anomalies.
  5. Remove the best year and test a stable parameter neighborhood.
  6. Document what the October 2026 universe prevents you from claiming.
