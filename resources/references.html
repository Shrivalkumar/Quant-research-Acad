How claims are labeled

Harness fact

Directly established by supplied code/data. These claims govern assignment results.

Official mechanism

Exchange or library documentation. Current market rules may differ from the historical pack.

Empirical finding

A result documented in a cited sample—not a promise or guaranteed trading rule.

Practitioner judgment

A useful research convention whose suitability depends on context and implementation.
