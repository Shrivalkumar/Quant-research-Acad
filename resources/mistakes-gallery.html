Research Mistakes Gallery

Twelve attractive mistakes that can make a backtest look better while making the evidence worse.

BAD RESEARCH

Centered moving average

A centered window at t uses observations after t.

Correct principle

Use a trailing window and verify the last value on truncated data.

BAD RESEARCH

Trading the signal close

The feature uses close t and the backtest also earns open→close t.

Correct principle

Apply the target from the next eligible open.

BAD RESEARCH

Ranking before eligibility

NaNs and stale/illiquid names receive unstable ranks.

Correct principle

Create a point-in-time eligibility mask, then rank only valid scores.

BAD RESEARCH

Shift on the wrong axis

prices.shift(1, axis=1) moves symbols, not time.

Correct principle

Shift rows; unit-test with labeled toy data.

BAD RESEARCH

Full-sample z-score

Mean and standard deviation include the future.

Correct principle

Use rolling or expanding estimates available at each date.

BAD RESEARCH

Backward fill

A missing historical value is replaced by a future observation.

Correct principle

Keep it missing or use justified forward fill after the source exists.

BAD RESEARCH

Survivor universe denial

A daily liquidity rule is said to remove selection bias.

Correct principle

Acknowledge that missing delisted/merged names cannot be recovered by filtering survivors.

BAD RESEARCH

Ignoring cost on flips

A +1→−1 change is charged as one side.

Correct principle

Use absolute position change: the notional change is two.

BAD RESEARCH

Stops filled at trigger after a gap

A buy stop at ₹100 fills at ₹100 despite opening at ₹106.

Correct principle

Use max(open, trigger), matching the harness.

BAD RESEARCH

Best-year removal by deleting trades

Trades crossing year boundaries are inconsistently removed.

Correct principle

Define the method on daily equity returns and state it consistently.

BAD RESEARCH

Reporting only the winning grid cell

Sensitivity testing becomes another optimization.

Correct principle

Show the whole predeclared grid and interpret the surface.

BAD RESEARCH

Invented failed ideas

A polished narrative is written without a saved run.

Correct principle

Log hypothesis and actual measured output; an unrun idea is not a failed experiment.
