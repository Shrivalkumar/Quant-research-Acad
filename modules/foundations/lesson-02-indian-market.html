Understanding the Indian Stock Market

Build a precise mental model of exchanges, the Nifty 50, index weights, sessions, and benchmarking.

Intuition first

An index is a measurement rule, not a tradeable share

NSE and BSE are marketplaces where listed securities trade under exchange rules. The Nifty 50 is calculated by NSE Indices from 50 large, liquid NSE-listed companies. It is a number describing a rules-based basket, not a company with shares. Exposure can be obtained indirectly through an index fund, ETF, or derivative, each with different mechanics.

Nifty 50 uses free-float market capitalization. Full market capitalization is shares outstanding times price. Free float removes strategic holdings considered unavailable for public trading, using an investible weight factor. A company with twice the free-float market value generally has twice the index influence. The divisor is adjusted for constituent changes and corporate actions so those administrative changes do not create fake index returns.

“Spot” in this assignment means observed Nifty index levels derived from cash-market constituent prices. The Task 1 position is economically modeled as a futures position using those spot bars plus explicit carry and roll assumptions. That is not the same as having actual futures quotes: basis can vary intraday and real contract execution has lot sizes, margin, and order-book effects.

A benchmark gives context. If a long-only stock strategy earns 50% while Nifty earns 45%, the five-point difference is not automatically skill: the strategy may have taken more small-cap, concentration, volatility, or survivor risk. Compare both return and risk, and be explicit that the pack’s Nifty series is a price index rather than dividend-adjusted total return unless established otherwise.

Questions beginners actually ask

Clear answers before we add mathematics

Is Nifty 50 a company or a share I can buy?

No. Nifty 50 is a calculated number that follows a rules-based basket of 50 companies. You can obtain similar exposure through an index fund, an exchange-traded fund, or a futures contract, but each is a separate financial product with its own price, costs, and mechanics.

If Nifty is at 20,000, does the basket cost ₹20,000?

No. The index level is a measurement relative to a base value, not a rupee purchase price. A fund can issue units at a completely different price, and a futures contract has a notional value determined by the index level and contract multiplier.

Why do large companies affect Nifty more?

Nifty 50 uses free-float market-capitalization weights. Roughly speaking, the publicly tradeable part of a company with twice the market value receives about twice the influence. A 1% move in a 10% weight contributes about 0.10 percentage points to the index; a 1% move in a 1% weight contributes about 0.01 points.

Does Nifty include dividends?

The commonly quoted Nifty 50 Price Index measures constituent price changes. NSE Indices also publishes a Total Return Index that assumes dividends are reinvested. A benchmark comparison must say which version is being used.

Follow the mechanism slowly

What happens first, second, third, and fourth

01

Companies issue ownership units

A listed company has shares. Trading a share changes who owns that unit; it does not directly change the company’s bank balance in ordinary secondary-market trading.

02

Exchanges produce observable prices

Orders on NSE or BSE meet and form traded prices. Index providers use eligible exchange prices—not managers’ opinions—to calculate their indices.

03

The index applies selection and weights

Nifty 50 selects 50 companies under its methodology and weights them by free-float market capitalization. A larger publicly tradeable market value receives more influence.

04

Products reference the calculation

Funds try to hold a matching basket; futures and options reference the index under derivative rules. Tracking the same index does not make these products mechanically identical.

Nifty 50 from first principles

Start with five objects that beginners often mix together

1 · Company

Reliance Industries, HDFC Bank, or another listed business is a legal company that earns revenue, owns assets, has liabilities, and issues shares.

2 · Share

A share is one ownership unit in a company. It has a rupee market price because buyers and sellers trade it on an exchange.

3 · Exchange

NSE and BSE are organized marketplaces. They match orders and publish prices under regulatory and clearing rules. They do not decide that every company should rise or fall.

4 · Index

Nifty 50 is a formula-driven measurement based on 50 selected NSE-listed companies. The index level is points, not a share certificate and not a rupee account balance.

5 · Product linked to the index

An index fund, ETF, option, or future is a separate product designed to follow or reference the index. It can be bought or traded; the mathematical index itself cannot.

Step-by-step calculation

How fifty company prices become one Nifty number

Step 1 — find each company’s market value. Market capitalization means share price multiplied by the number of shares outstanding. If a company has 10 crore shares and each trades at ₹200, its full market capitalization is ₹2,000 crore.

Step 2 — keep only the free-float portion. Suppose promoters or strategic owners hold 60% and only 40% is treated as publicly available. The investible weight factor is 0.40. Free-float market capitalization is ₹2,000 crore × 0.40 = ₹800 crore.

Step 3 — repeat for every constituent. Add the free-float market capitalizations of all 50 companies. A company’s approximate weight is its free-float value divided by the total. If the total is ₹8,000 crore in a toy index, the ₹800 crore company has a 10% weight.

Step 4 — translate the basket value into index points. The official calculation uses a base market capitalization and base index value, together with divisor adjustments. The base exists so the index forms a continuous measurement series. Nifty 50’s methodology uses a base date of 3 November 1995 and base value of 1,000. An index at 20,000 therefore means the calculated basket measure is twenty times its base level under the maintained methodology; it does not mean “pay ₹20,000 to buy Nifty.”

Step 5 — maintain continuity. Constituents can change and companies can split shares, merge, or issue securities. Index maintenance adjusts shares or the divisor so an administrative change does not create a fake market return. The goal is for index changes to represent market-price movement rather than bookkeeping.

Toy contribution: if a 10%-weight company rises 3% while every other constituent is unchanged, its approximate contribution is 10% × 3% = 0.30 percentage points. The index would rise about 0.30%, not 3%.

This distinction matters in performance comparisons. A long-only stock portfolio owns companies that may pay dividends. Comparing it with a price-only index can flatter the portfolio if dividends are included on one side but not the other. NSE, BSE, Sensex, Nifty, Bank Nifty

A map of the Indian market names

NSE and BSE are exchanges. Nifty 50 is NSE Indices’ broad flagship 50-stock index. Sensex is BSE’s well-known 30-stock benchmark. Bank Nifty is a sector index focused on banking companies; it is not a second name for Nifty 50. A bank-sector index is naturally more concentrated in interest rates, credit cycles, and banking regulation than a multi-sector index.

Official NSE material describes Nifty 50 as a diversified 50-stock index spanning 13 sectors and, as of 30 March 2026, representing about 53.73% of the free-float market capitalization of NSE-listed stocks. Those percentages change with prices and methodology reviews, so treat them as dated context—not timeless constants.

A normal NSE cash-market session is commonly described from 09:15 to 15:30 IST, but your Task 1 file is not a live feed. Its 15-minute bars are labeled by their start time: 09:15, 09:30, …, 15:15. The 15:15 bar covers the final interval. One supplied Muhurat session has only three bars. Always follow the data and harness rather than assuming every day has an identical row count.

The exact assignment connection

Why the Nifty lesson is necessary before Task 1

  1. The CSV contains Nifty 50 spot-index OHLC. These are calculated index levels, not futures trade prices and not 50 separate stock rows.
  2. Your function studies those spot bars. It can use only bars available through the current close.
  3. Your function outputs a target direction. +1 means modeled long futures exposure, 0 flat, and −1 modeled short exposure.
  4. The harness fills at the next bar’s open. This prevents the close used by the signal from also becoming an unrealistically available earlier fill.
  5. The harness adds futures economics separately. Spot price returns are combined with modeled carry, cash interest, transaction cost, and monthly roll cost.

The model is useful for the assignment because it gives every candidate the same accounting. Its limitation is equally important: real futures basis, order-book liquidity, lot sizes, margin calls, taxes, and contract-specific prices are simplified or absent. A good report explains what was tested under the harness without claiming it perfectly reproduces live trading.

Worked walkthrough

A three-company index

Float market caps are ₹500m, ₹750m, and ₹750m; total = ₹2,000m. Weights are 25%, 37.5%, and 37.5%. If A rises 4% while B and C are unchanged, the index return is approximately 0.25×4% = 1%, ignoring divisor rounding. A ₹150 stock is not necessarily “larger” than a ₹50 stock.

Failure prevention

Common mistakes

  • Calling the index directly tradeable
  • Weighting constituents by share price alone
  • Comparing a price index to dividend-reinvested equity returns without disclosure
  • Hard-coding 25 bars per day despite the pack’s short session
Retrieval practice

Exercises

1. Which most directly determines a Nifty constituent’s weight?

  1. Its share price only
  2. Its float-adjusted market capitalization
  3. Alphabetical order
  4. Daily volume only
Reveal explanation

Answer: B. The methodology is float-adjusted market-cap weighting.

2. Why is Nifty spot not identical to Nifty futures?

  1. Futures are company shares
  2. Futures have expiry and basis/carry
  3. Spot trades only monthly
  4. There is no relation
Reveal explanation

Answer: B. A dated derivative can differ from spot through financing, dividends, demand, and time to expiry.

3. A strategy beats Nifty by five points. What can you conclude immediately?

  1. It has alpha
  2. It took less risk
  3. Only that its measured return was five points higher over that window
  4. It will win hidden data
Reveal explanation

Answer: C. Attribution and risk are required before claiming skill.
